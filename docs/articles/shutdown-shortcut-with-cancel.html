A shutdown shortcut you can cancel

A shutdown button on the desktop is handy until you hit it by accident and lose unsaved work. A good shutdown shortcut gives you a few seconds to change your mind.

Option 1. A shortcut with the shutdown command

  1. Right-click the desktop → New → Shortcut.
  2. For the location, type: shutdown /s /t 30
  3. Name it “Shut down” and click Finish.

The PC shuts down after 30 seconds. To cancel, you need a second shortcut with shutdown /a, or type it quickly into Win + R. It works, but cancelling is awkward and there is no countdown on screen.

Option 2. A button with a countdown and cancel

In One Click Switcher, the Shut down button shows a 5-second countdown window with a soft sound. Cancel any natural way:

  • the Cancel button;
  • Esc or Space;
  • a click on the countdown window;
  • clicking the same icon again.

Restart works the same way. No second shortcut, no commands.

Which one to choose

For a 30- or 60-minute timer, the shutdown command with the right number of seconds is fine. For a plain “shut down now” button that forgives a misclick, the countdown button is easier.
