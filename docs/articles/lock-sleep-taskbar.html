Lock and Sleep buttons on the Windows 10 and 11 taskbar

Stepping away? Lock the PC. Leaving for a while? Put it to sleep. Here are the fastest ways.

Lock

The fastest is Win + L. But many people don't know it or forget it when their hand is on the mouse.

Sleep

Built-in path: Start → power icon → Sleep. Three clicks and a bit of aiming. You can also set the case power button to Sleep in Control Panel → Power Options.

Sleep vs shut down

Sleep keeps your open apps and documents as they are and wakes in a couple of seconds. Shut down closes everything. Away for an hour or two — sleep; overnight — shut down.
