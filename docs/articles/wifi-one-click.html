How to quickly turn off Wi-Fi on Windows 11

You may want Wi-Fi off when the PC is on a cable, when the kids should take a break from the internet, or when the connection hangs and needs a restart.

Option 1. Quick Settings

Press Win + A and click the Wi-Fi tile. Same again to turn it back on.

Option 2. Settings

Win + I → Network & internet → the Wi-Fi switch.

Option 3. A taskbar button

With One Click Switcher, Wi-Fi is one taskbar button. It is especially handy on a wired PC: the network icon shows the cable, so you can't see whether Wi-Fi is on. The Status button shows Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, microphone and sound at a glance.
